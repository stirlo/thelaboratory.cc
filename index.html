About this workspace

Built by TFP. Maintained as projects move from idea to testable system.

The Laboratory is TFP's public workbench: a place for useful software, strange ideas, and the evidence left behind while building them.

Projects appear here at different stages. A listing may point to a finished tool, an actively maintained system, or a proof of concept kept for reference.
